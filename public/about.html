About Medi-Vault

Moving healthcare from paper files locked in one hospital to records that follow the patient.

Healthcare in India is going digital fast. Electronic health records make care safer and better coordinated: doctors see a patient's history at a glance, prescriptions are clear, and nothing is lost between visits.

Yet most clinical data still sits in silos. Each hospital keeps its own files, and patients rarely get access to them. They are left juggling paper reports from every doctor they have ever seen.

Medi-Vault changes that. Any hospital can use it. Doctors record each visit, and patients can see every record written for them, from every hospital, in one place.

How it works

Three simple steps from the consultation room to the patient's screen.

Doctor joins their hospital

A doctor signs up and picks the hospital they work at. Each doctor belongs to exactly one hospital.

Doctor records the visit

After a consultation, the doctor adds the diagnosis, vitals, prescription and next visit date, along with the patient's email.

Patient sees their records

The patient signs up with that same email and instantly sees every record any doctor has added for them.

Privacy comes first

Medical records are sensitive. Access is checked by the database itself, not only by the website.

Only the right people

Doctors see only the records they wrote. Patients see only records with their confirmed email.

Full edit history

Records are never deleted. Every edit keeps a copy of the previous version.

Verified accounts

Every account confirms its email address before it can log in.
